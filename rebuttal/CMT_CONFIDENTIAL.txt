Three procedural notes for the chairs; none of them is a complaint about a review.

1. Self-disclosed defect. Reviewer #2 inspected our released code and correctly found that the
calibration split was used both to choose and to certify the deployed plan. In auditing the
rest of the data flow for the response we found a second deviation of the same kind that no
review raised -- the fallback plan was also screened using calibration data -- and we have
disclosed it in the public response rather than leave it for a later reader. Both deviations
are from the paper's own Proposition 2, which states the correct policy; re-running under the
policy the paper describes leaves the headline certified bound numerically identical (0.2629)
and slightly lowers the realized violation. We would rather the committee weigh this knowing
we reported the part that was not asked about.

2. Because ICDE 2027 has no revision phase, we implemented the editorial items rather than
promising them. All 24 edits corresponding to Reviewer #1's D1-D14 and to the figure and
page-6 formatting faults are applied and the manuscript recompiles cleanly; the edit script,
the diff and the rebuilt PDF are in the public artifact. We are not asking for these to count
as a revision -- only that the reviewers can verify the faults are understood and fixable, and
that none of them touches a result.

3. Artifact access. Reviewer #3 reported being unable to reach the repository URL printed in
the paper. It is public and we have re-verified anonymous access; we cannot reconstruct the
failure. Separately, the release was partial by design at submission, which was our own
mistake, and it is now complete. If it would help, we are glad to send the chairs a self-
contained archive: every number in the response is reproducible from the included compressed
action rows on CPU alone, without a GPU or any download.
